Character Relic setup

I put all ten Elden Ring Nightreign Nightfarers in one table at level 15, with the base stats and the relic setups next to each other, so I can sort by DEX, STR, INT, FAI, ARC, VIG, END or MND and pick a character without alt tabbing to a wiki.

The numbers are my own notes from the game and community wiki pages, so a wrong value is possible. Nothing leaves your browser.

Provided free of charge, without tracking. For the love of the game.
